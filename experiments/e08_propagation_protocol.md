# E08 · Propagating fraction uncertainty into weak-overlap risk

Protocol recorded before fitting. Use the existing 263 Burn Scars patches, five geographic folds, assignment rules, threshold 0.10, 15-neighbor eligibility, seed 808 and 100 paired positive/shuffled-error draws. Keep the original pilot outputs. Fit fraction models to all other-fold patches and evaluate both risks on exactly the same apparently adequate held-out patches as E08. The fixed assignment rules and geographic partition are design information. No simulated treatment or outcome is required.

1. Fit positive-beta regressions with the E05/E07 logit mean, log precision and Gaussian coefficient priors. Use standardized log1p(100 × proxy) in both components. Add density in both components in the second model. Use four cohort-wide density rank groups (0 to 3 as a standardized ordered covariate) for an identifiable comparison with the finite-population benchmark below. Cut points use density alone. Also fit continuous log density on the real proxy to check sensitivity to grouping. Integrate exact beta tail probabilities over 128 fixed scrambled Sobol Gaussian draws from the Laplace approximation. Check the real results against the original importance-sampling posterior on all five folds. Report held-out fraction log predictive density (higher is better) and Brier score for weak overlap (lower is better). Pool patches and also report each fold with its hidden count.
2. Synthetic benchmark: consider a uniformly sampled patch from the fixed 263-member population. Sum the known proxy likelihood over all candidate reference fractions, including the boundary sign reversal. For the positive arm use each candidate's true probability of large error and for the negative arm use its unconditional single-patch probability (the average mixture weight). Condition first on proxy alone and then on proxy plus the observed density quarter. This gives the exact marginal conditional risks for these observables under this finite-population experiment. It deliberately uses the population reference bank, including held-out patches, and never uses the identity of the query patch. Exact continuous densities would identify individual patches in this empirical population. The benchmark does not condition on other patches' simulated proxies, eligibility, or fold. In particular it is a privileged diagnostic, not deployable or a guaranteed Brier bound after eligibility selection.
3. Compare density-minus-proxy Brier changes within each arm and paired differences between arms. Report average magnitudes, Monte Carlo SEs for paired contrasts, and frequencies of improvement and paired wins. Retain the preceding sensitivity convention (at least 80/100 positive improvements and 80/100 paired wins per rule). A stronger known-mechanism contrast than learned contrast suggests incomplete recovery of a signal available under the design; little contrast even there suggests limited incremental information from the injected mechanism. The negative arm retains geographic associations of density with the reference. Real-data conclusions remain exploratory and conditional on the cohort. Report both rules even when they disagree.
