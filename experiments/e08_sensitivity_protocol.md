# E08 overlap sensitivity protocol

Recorded before running the variants. Use the original 263 burn-scar test patches, five geographic folds, two assignment rules, 0.10 threshold and diagnostic models. Treatment probabilities always depend only on reference fraction. Retain the original E08 outputs and check the real-proxy results at 15 neighbors against them. Run 5, 15 and 30 neighbors with all boundary ties throughout, including inner cross-fitting.

1. Simulate 100 paired proxy draws (NumPy seed 808). Each patch independently draws a small absolute error uniformly in [0, 0.02] or a large error uniformly in [0.10, 0.25]. The chance of large error declines linearly from 0.95 to 0.05 across ascending density ranks. Randomly permute these exact magnitudes across patches for the negative control. Share fair random signs across arms, reversing an infeasible sign to keep fractions in [0,1]. This makes the negative absolute error independent of density by design, with boundary-dependent signs conditional on C. Check pooled MAE equality and the realized low-versus-other density MAE in each draw. This is a deliberately strong heteroscedastic signal.
2. Use the pilot's held-out Brier difference (with density minus proxy only, smaller is better). For each rule and neighborhood, report how often the positive control improves, how often the negative improves and how often the paired positive difference is smaller than the negative difference, counting strict wins. Report the latter's binomial Monte Carlo standard error. Under this diagnostic convention, at least 80% paired wins plus improvement in at least 80% of positive draws indicates clear sensitivity to this injected mechanism. A negative control may still benefit from density because C and geography remain associated with density. Win frequency is descriptive, not a calibrated hypothesis test.
3. On the real proxy, predict absolute fraction error out of geographic fold with a ridge regression (alpha 1) using the same standardized proxy spline, then add log density. Compare held-out MAE overall and by fold. For direction, compare hidden cases with other reference-weak patches on absolute and signed proxy error. Compute the direct counterfactual overlap obtained by substituting the proxy in the fixed assignment rule, and rerun the identical held-out smoother using C itself as its input. Count direct crossings and transitions between proxy-smoothed and reference-smoothed hidden status. The reference smoother is an oracle diagnostic of smoothing and geographic extrapolation.
4. Report patch-weighted and fold-level results, hidden counts and eligible denominators in both arms and on real data. Treat a real incremental benefit as robust only if its direction agrees across both rules and all three neighborhoods; inspect fold concentration. Weak control sensitivity limits interpretation of a null real result. Successful controls plus little real gain support a cohort-specific signal limitation. Retain mixed results without selecting a preferred setting after inspection.
